How an order comes together

  1. 01 Design
    Send a photo, a name, or an idea. We sketch the layout and confirm size, wood, and finish before anything is cut.
  2. 02 Cut & engrave
    Each piece is laser-cut and engraved to the confirmed design, checked by hand at every stage.
  3. 03 Finish & deliver
    Sanded, sealed, and packed carefully. Most orders are ready within a week, faster for simple pieces.
